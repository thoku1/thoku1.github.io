Reservations and Fares

Vehicle reservations are recommended during the summer months. Foot passengers do not need a reservation. A single foot passenger ticket on the Tau route costs 68 NOK. Vehicles under 6 metres cost 245 NOK including the driver. Bicycles are carried free of charge. Holders of public transport season cards receive a 20 % discount as foot passengers.

Boarding

Vehicles should arrive at least 15 minutes before departure. Vehicles with a reservation are loaded first.
