TAP Air Portugal Baggage Policy 2026

This page summarizes the checked baggage policy of TAP Air Portugal for 2026, including allowances by fare class, weight and size limits, and excess baggage fees. It is intended as a quick reference for travelers and travel agents.

Checked Baggage Allowance by Fare Class

  • Economy Discount: 1 checked bag up to 23 kg (50 lbs)
  • Economy Classic: 1 checked bag up to 23 kg (50 lbs)
  • Economy Plus: 2 checked bags up to 23 kg (50 lbs) each
  • Economy Prime: 2 checked bags up to 23 kg (50 lbs) each
  • Business Class: 2 checked bags up to 32 kg (70 lbs) each
  • Infants: 1 bag up to 10 kg plus a stroller

Size Limits and Excess Baggage

Each checked bag must not exceed 158 cm (62 inches) in total dimensions. Overweight bags (23-32 kg) and oversized bags incur additional fees. During high season periods on certain routes, TAP may not allow additional checked baggage purchases beyond the fare allowance.

Carry-On Allowance

Economy passengers are allowed one carry-on bag (up to 8 kg on most routes) plus one personal item. Business Class passengers receive a higher carry-on allowance.
