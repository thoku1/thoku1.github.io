Trondheim Tram Extension 2026 - Line 9 Timetable and Zone Changes

The Trondheim tram network was extended in 2026 with a new eastern branch of Line 9. The extension adds four stops and shifts part of the route from zone B into zone A, which reduces the fare for passengers travelling from the eastern suburbs toward the centre.

Timetable

  • Weekdays: every 10 minutes between 06:00 and 19:00, every 20 minutes in the evening
  • Saturday: every 15 minutes between 09:00 and 18:00
  • Sunday: every 20 minutes, first departure 08:30

New Stops

The four new stops are Lademoen, Buran Vest, Rosenborg Øst and Valen. All four have step-free access and platform readers that accept the same contactless card as the rest of the network.

Zone and Fare Changes

Buran Vest and Rosenborg Øst lie in zone A following the change, while Lademoen and Valen remain in zone B. Passengers who previously bought a zone A + B ticket for a journey ending at Rosenborg Øst may now travel on a zone A ticket alone.

Connections

Line 9 connects with the bus network at Lademoen and with the regional rail at the central station. A single ticket remains valid for 90 minutes across all modes within the purchased zones.
