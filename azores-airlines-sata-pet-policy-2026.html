Azores Airlines (SATA) Pet Policy 2026

Azores Airlines (SATA Air Açores) allows small dogs and cats to travel in the cabin on inter-island and international flights, subject to carrier and destination requirements. This page summarizes the current rules, fees and documentation needed for traveling with pets on SATA.

In-Cabin Pet Requirements

  • Eligible animals: small dogs and cats, minimum age 8 weeks
  • Carrier: soft-sided carrier that fits under the seat in front of you (max dimensions 40 x 30 x 20 cm)
  • Weight limit: combined weight of pet and carrier must not exceed 8 kg (17 lbs)
  • Capacity: maximum two pets per flight, accepted on a first-come, first-served basis at check-in
  • Not permitted: in-cabin pets on flights to Canada; other international destinations may require a health certificate issued within 10 days of travel

Pet Fees (2026)

A pet fee of 75 EUR applies per flight segment for in-cabin travel, payable at check-in. Pets traveling in the cargo hold are charged separately according to weight.

Carry-On Rules for Other Pets

Emotional support animals are no longer granted automatic cabin access on Azores Airlines flights. Service dogs trained to assist passengers with disabilities may travel free of charge in the cabin, subject to advance notice.
