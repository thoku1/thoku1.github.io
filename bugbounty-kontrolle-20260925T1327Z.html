Innsbruck Airport Winter Baggage Rules 2026

Innsbruck Airport (INN) handles a large volume of winter sports traffic between December and March. The rules below apply to scheduled and charter departures from the main terminal and cover ski and snowboard equipment, which is treated as a separate category from ordinary checked baggage.

Standard Allowance

  • Carry-on: one piece, max 55 x 40 x 20 cm, max 8 kg
  • Checked: depends on fare; most winter charters include one piece up to 20 kg
  • Ski equipment: one ski bag plus one boot bag count together as a single checked item on most carriers

Ski and Snowboard Carriage

A ski bag may contain one pair of skis, one pair of poles and one pair of ski boots. Snowboards are carried in a dedicated board bag; a board bag and a separate boot bag are counted as two items unless the carrier states otherwise. Helmets travel free of charge when packed inside the equipment bag.

Excess and Oversized Fees

Equipment exceeding 158 cm in total dimensions is charged at the oversized rate. Fees vary by carrier and are normally lower when paid online in advance rather than at the check-in desk. Passengers connecting through Munich or Zurich should confirm whether the equipment is through-checked or must be reclaimed and re-checked.

Seasonal Notes

Between 20 December and 6 January, oversized baggage drop-off moves to the separate counter in the arrivals hall to reduce queueing at the main desks. Allow an additional 45 minutes during this period.
