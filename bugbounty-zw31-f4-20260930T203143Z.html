Reservations and Fares

Vehicle reservations are recommended during the summer months. Foot passengers do not need a reservation. A single foot passenger ticket on the Tau route costs 68 NOK. Vehicles under 6 metres cost 245 NOK including the driver. Bicycles are carried free of charge.
