Stavanger Ferry Timetable 2026 - Ryfylke Routes

The Ryfylke ferry routes connect Stavanger with the fjord communities to the north and east. Services run year-round, with increased frequency between May and September. Vehicle spaces should be booked in advance during the summer months; foot passengers do not require a reservation.

Routes and Crossing Times

  • Stavanger - Tau: 40 minutes, departures every 30 minutes on weekdays
  • Stavanger - Hjelmeland: 1 hour 20 minutes, four departures daily
  • Stavanger - Lysebotn: seasonal service, May to September only

Fares

Foot passengers pay 68 NOK for a single crossing on the Tau route. Vehicles under 6 metres pay 245 NOK including the driver. Bicycles are carried free of charge on all routes. Passengers holding a period pass for the bus network receive a 20 percent discount on foot passenger fares.

Vehicle Carriage and Loading

Vehicles should arrive at least 15 minutes before departure. Loading is first-come, first-served for unbooked vehicles, but booked vehicles are loaded first. Motorhomes and vehicles over 6 metres must be declared at the time of booking.

Onward Connections

At Tau the ferry connects with the bus network toward Hjelmeland and Årdal. At Hjelmeland there is a connecting service to the northern fjord villages. Bicycles may be taken on all connecting buses subject to space.
