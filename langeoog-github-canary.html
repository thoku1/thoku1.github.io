Langeoog Island Railway: Luggage Transport & Barrier-Free Travel 2026

Langeoog is a car-free North Sea island. The island railway (Inselbahn) connects the ferry landing with the village — a 2.5 km ride included in your ferry ticket. This page is a practical reference for travellers who need to know how luggage transport works, especially those with limited mobility or travelling for a spa stay.

How Luggage Gets from Ferry to Village

At the Bensersiel ferry terminal on the mainland, you load your bags into designated luggage containers. Staff will help if you ask. The luggage travels separately — you board the ferry carrying only hand luggage, and your checked bags are transferred to the island railway and delivered to the village station. One suitcase per person (up to 30 kg) is included in the ferry fare.

Deadline: bags must be loaded at least 10 minutes before the scheduled departure.

Reduced Mobility

The red carriages of the island railway have automatic doors and ramps for wheelchairs and prams. A wheelchair can be reserved in advance at the station or at the Bensersiel ferry terminal.

The official Langeoog accessibility page recommends driving to the ferry terminal first, dropping off your luggage, and then parking your car — so you never need to carry heavy bags across the car park.

If you cannot cycle or walk longer distances, contact the Langeoog tourism service before you travel. They arrange transport from the station to your accommodation. On a car-free island this is routine — it just needs booking ahead.

Door-to-Door Delivery

Gepäckdienst Heyken is a private luggage delivery service on Langeoog, active as of summer 2026. They collect your bags and bring them directly to your hotel or holiday rental — ideal if carrying any distance is a challenge. Pre-booking recommended.
