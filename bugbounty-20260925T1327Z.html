Bergen Light Rail Zone Fares 2026

The Bergen light rail network (Bybanen) uses a zone-based fare system shared with the city bus network. A single ticket is valid for unlimited transfers within its zone coverage for 90 minutes from the moment of activation. Tickets must be activated in the app or validated at a platform reader before boarding.

Zones and Single Tickets

  • Zone A (city centre): single ticket 44 NOK
  • Zone A + B: single ticket 79 NOK
  • All zones: single ticket 116 NOK
  • Airport line (Line 1 to Flesland): an additional supplement of 55 NOK applies on top of the zone fare

Period Passes

Seven-day passes are available for Zone A (280 NOK), Zone A + B (490 NOK) and all zones (700 NOK). Thirty-day passes cost roughly three times the seven-day rate. Students and seniors receive a 50 percent discount on all period products with valid documentation. Children under 6 travel free of charge; children aged 6 to 15 travel at half fare.

Transfers and Validity

A single ticket covers transfers between light rail, city bus and the Bergen commuter rail within the same zones for 90 minutes. Night services after 01:00 require a new ticket. Passengers travelling to the airport should note that the supplement is charged on exit as well as entry, so the ticket must remain valid for the whole journey.
